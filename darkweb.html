CONNECTION

A collector connects Tor to your feed

  1. Run Tor and the CT Atlas collector on a dedicated machine or your computer.
  2. The collector reads the outlets registered by the administrator.
  3. It follows their internal pages and pagination through Tor and sends metadata to CT Atlas over authenticated HTTPS.
  4. This workspace displays new links and keyword alerts. Your computer does not need Tor to read the feed.

Checks run every 15 minutes while the collector is running. A pass checks up to 100 pages by default and resumes next time. The first complete inventory establishes a baseline and does not trigger new-material alerts.

COLLECTION SCOPE

Metadata first, evidence on your collector

This version progressively follows reachable internal pages on registered onion hosts, preserving its queue between passes. It does not crawl the whole dark web or bypass logins.

Optional acquisition downloads new PDF, video, audio and image links to the collector’s local evidence directory, with a SHA-256 content hash and a size cap. Original files are not hosted on this website.

The feed retains the latest 500 discoveries. A URL fingerprint identifies a link; a content hash is available only after acquisition. A new link is a discovery lead, not proof of a new publication.
