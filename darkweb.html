Dark Web News Feed

The content below is the CT ATLAS Dark Web News Feed: publications and news released by terrorist-linked groups and collected daily from selected encrypted forums and publication outlets on the Dark Web. The collection currently goes back to 1 January 2026. Collection is managed centrally by the administrator; analysts only need to review the feed below. Original-language text is preserved, English translations and overviews are added when available, and associated PDFs or other collected files remain attached to the relevant publication. Source claims require analyst verification.

Collection controls

SAVE keeps everything already collected. It can widen the period (an earlier From or a later Through); outlets then re-crawl to collect the newly covered dates. Narrowing the period requires RESET, which deletes the whole archive.

Structured publications are retained in the paginated archive. Their PDF attachments are downloaded on the collector computer, up to 2 per pass and the configured file-size cap, then sent to private Atlas storage when activated. Other original media are not downloaded automatically. Undated records remain in local review. Previews: up to 2 per pass, 8 MB per source.

Private PDF storage

The limit applies to PDFs sent by this collector. Reaching it stops new uploads and keeps local files. Existing PDFs remain accessible. Other Cloudflare account usage is separate.

CONNECTION

A collector connects Tor to your feed

  1. Run Tor and the CT Atlas collector on a dedicated machine or your computer.
  2. The collector reads the outlets registered by the administrator.
  3. It follows their internal pages and pagination through Tor and sends metadata to CT Atlas over authenticated HTTPS.
  4. This workspace displays new links and keyword alerts. Your computer does not need Tor to read the feed.

Checks run every 15 minutes while the collector is running. A pass checks up to 10 pages by default and resumes next time. After the initial inventory, watch checks the starting page and links within two levels; this is not a guarantee of complete ongoing coverage. The first complete inventory establishes a baseline and does not trigger new-material alerts.

COLLECTION SCOPE

Publications and private PDF access

This version progressively follows reachable internal pages on registered onion hosts, preserving its queue between passes. It does not crawl the whole dark web or bypass logins.

Structured PDF attachments are preserved locally, then synchronized with private Atlas storage (up to 50 MiB per PDF). Uploaded PDFs can be opened and downloaded after signing in to Atlas. Existing local PDFs can be synchronized without restarting collection. Other original media remain local.

The publication archive preserves structured records separately from the latest 500 feed discoveries. A URL fingerprint identifies a link; a content hash is available only after acquisition. A new link is a discovery lead, not proof of a new publication.
