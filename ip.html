05 / INVESTIGATOR HANDOFF

Prepare the provider enquiry

  1. Preserve the original log, public IP, exact timestamp and timezone, source port and protocol where available.
  2. Check whether the observed address is an access network, a hosting service, a VPN exit or another intermediary. Confirm the responsible provider.
  3. Use the provider's current official procedure through your agency. A network registration or contact result does not establish subscriber identity.
