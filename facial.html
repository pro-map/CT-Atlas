FACE CROPS
Cut, download and search the detected faces
THIRD-PARTY SEARCH. Cutting, JPEG, ZIP and COPY happen only in your browser. To open an engine's results directly, SEARCH needs a web address for the image: after a confirmation, that one crop is hosted on CT Atlas's temporary storage for about 10 minutes (anyone holding its unguessable link can view it meanwhile), the engine downloads it (and applies its own retention rules to that copy), and CT Atlas then deletes its hosted copy automatically. Each further engine on the same crop asks for its own confirmation, and CT Atlas is not sent to the engines as the referring site. Search4faces, and any failure, use copy-and-paste instead: the face reaches that service only when you paste it there. Results are leads, not identifications (look-alikes are common), and none of these services is operated or verified by CT Atlas.

VISUAL SIMILARITY

Detection and similarity outputs are investigative aids only. Perceptual similarity describes image resemblance, not identity. Face detection does not establish who a person is, and matches returned by third-party reverse-image engines are unverified leads that require independent corroboration.
